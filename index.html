Our Mission

Meditation should be easier for everyone

GodAura Devices was founded in 2022 with one idea — meditation should be easier for everyone.

After seeing how many people struggled with stress, anxiety, insomnia, and constant overthinking, we wanted to create something that could genuinely help. Meditation has always been powerful, but many people find it difficult to quiet their minds and fully relax.

That inspired the idea behind GodAura: a wearable device that uses carefully tuned rhythmic vibrations to help guide the brain into a calmer, more focused state.

Our Story

Learn More About GodAura

GodAura Devices was born from a deeply personal experience. Our founder spent years watching friends and family struggle with stress, anxiety, and sleepless nights — trying everything from meditation apps to breathing exercises, but never quite finding something that truly quieted the mind.

How It Works

GodAura devices use rhythmic haptic vibrations — precisely tuned frequencies that gently guide your brainwaves into calmer states. Think of it like a metronome for your mind.

Vibration Patterns

Calibrated frequencies target theta and alpha brainwave states.

EEG Monitoring

Real-time brainwave tracking adapts vibration intensity.

AI Personalization

Machine learning creates custom sessions that evolve with you.

Our Vision

We envision a world where mental wellness is accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford therapists or retreats. GodAura is building technology that fits into your life, not the other way around. Whether you have 5 minutes or 50, our devices adapt to your schedule and needs.

The Team

Our team combines expertise in neuroscience, haptic engineering, industrial design, and clinical psychology. Based in San Francisco, we're a diverse group of 32 people united by the belief that technology should serve our wellbeing — not drain it.

What's Next

In 2025, we're launching the GodAura App 2.0 with social meditation features, the AuraMax VR integration for immersive experiences, and partnerships with 50+ wellness clinics across North America. We're also beginning clinical trials for GodAura's potential in supporting PTSD treatment.

Our Research Library

Peer-reviewed studies and real-user data that validate the impact of GodAura technology.

Published — Journal of Neurotherapy, 2023

Haptic Vibration Therapy and Cortisol Reduction: A Randomized Controlled Trial

A 200-participant, 8-week randomized controlled trial demonstrating that GodAura's rhythmic vibration patterns produced a statistically significant 42% average reduction in salivary cortisol levels compared to the control group (p < 0.001).

200 Participants 8 Weeks 42% Cortisol Decrease
Published — Sleep Medicine Reviews, 2024

Wearable Haptic Stimulation for Insomnia: Efficacy and Sleep Architecture Changes

Polysomnography study showing that nightly use of GodAura devices increased deep sleep duration by an average of 38 minutes and reduced sleep onset latency by 22 minutes in participants with chronic insomnia.

120 Participants 12 Weeks +38min Deep Sleep
Published — Cognitive Neuroscience, 2024

Adaptive Vibration Feedback and Sustained Attention: An EEG-Based Analysis

EEG-monitored study demonstrating that GodAura's adaptive vibration feedback improved sustained attention scores by 37% during focused work tasks, with increased alpha-wave coherence in prefrontal regions.

80 Participants 6 Weeks +37% Focus Score
In Progress — Stanford Center for Mindfulness, 2025

GodAura Vibration Therapy as Adjunctive Treatment for PTSD-Related Hyperarousal

Ongoing clinical trial investigating whether GodAura's haptic vibration patterns can reduce hyperarousal symptoms in individuals with PTSD, when used alongside traditional therapy. Expected completion: Q4 2025.

150 Participants 16 Weeks In Progress
