About GodAura

Pioneering the future of mindful technology through neuroscience and design.

Our Mission

Founded in 2022, GodAura Devices was born from a simple belief: meditation should be measurable, personal, and beautifully integrated into modern life. We combine clinical-grade biosensors with adaptive AI to create headbands that listen to your nervous system and guide you toward deeper states of calm and focus.

Our team of neuroscientists, engineers, and wellness designers work at the intersection of brain-computer interfaces and human-centered design — building tools that feel as premium as they perform.

◈ NEURAL ◈

Science Meets Serenity

Every GodAura device is validated through peer-reviewed protocols measuring HRV, EEG patterns, cortisol proxies, and subjective wellness scores. We partner with university labs and corporate wellness programs to continuously refine our algorithms.

From sleep optimization to executive focus training, our platform evolves with you — delivering insights through a calming, minimalist interface that respects your attention and your data privacy.
